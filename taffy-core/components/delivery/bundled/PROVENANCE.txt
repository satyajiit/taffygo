Why the bytes are committed rather than built
---------------------------------------------

Decision 0202 point 4 decides this and gives the reasoning; what follows is
that reasoning checked against this tree, because a provenance record that
restates a decision without verifying it is worth nothing.

Two of the four cannot be rebuilt on a clean checkout at all:

  * python-stdlib   is built from taffy-core/third_party/cpython/src/Lib,
                    which ./tools/chromium/sync unpacks from the pinned
                    tarball and this repository does not track. Measured:
                    `git ls-files 'taffy-core/third_party/cpython/src/*'`
                    returns nothing.
  * country-flags   commits no artwork at all. Its recipe fetches the pinned
                    flag-icons 7.5.0 tarball and rasterises it through
                    librsvg, cairo and cwebp. Measured: no tracked .svg and
                    no tracked .webp anywhere under
                    taffy-core/third_party/flag-icons/.

The other two **do** rebuild from the tree, and are committed anyway:

  * easylist-base   rebuilds byte-identically from the snapshots committed
                    under taffy-core/third_party/easylist/vendor/.
  * start-scenes    rebuilds from 24 committed WebP members under
                    taffy-core/ui/android/core/ui/vendor/start-scenes/webp/.
                    Measured: 24 tracked members, and the pack carries 25
                    entries — those 24 plus its own NOTICE.

They are committed because committing two and building two would be two
mechanisms for one kind of thing, which is decision 0202's own reason rather
than an inference drawn here.

One hazard applies to all four and would bite quietly if any of them were
rebuilt during the build instead. taffy-core/third_party/cpython/tools/
artifact_zip.py pins entry order, timestamps, permission bits, create_system
and COMPRESS_LEVEL = 9 — but a deflate *level* is not a deflate
*implementation*, and the bytes are still whatever the host's zlib writes. A
pack rebuilt elsewhere is a different digest for identical contents, and the
delivery plane checks the digest.

The artifacts
-------------

Four files, one per distinct blob:

  easylist-base.zip    easylist-base at revision 202608272347-taffy.1,
                       3 entries, carrying the android-arm64 and android-x64
                       variants
  stdlib.zip           python-stdlib at revision 3.14.7-taffy.1, 564 entries,
                       carrying both Android variants -- the catalogue
                       publishes this blob at two paths and they are the same
                       bytes, which is why there is one file here and not two
  flags-4x3-webp.zip   country-flags at revision 7.5.0-taffy.1, 250 entries,
                       carrying both Android variants
  scenes-4x3-webp.zip  start-scenes at revision 20260910-taffy.1, 25 entries,
                       carrying both Android variants

Two gates read these bytes and they answer different questions.

In the `files` lane, tools/lib/vendored_assets.py reads the `sha256_` and
`bytes_` lines at the end of this file against this directory, both ways: a
file whose bytes moved is a finding, a line naming a file that is not here is
a finding, and a fifth artifact dropped in beside these four with no line of
its own is a finding on the day it lands. This record carries no checksum of
itself, because writing the line would change the bytes it measures; the gate
excuses exactly this one file and nothing else in the directory.

In the `catalog` lane, `bundled_finding` in bundled_rows.py, beside the
asset-plane generator, reads the same files against the catalogue: each one's measured length and digest
must equal the `transfer_bytes` and `digest` of every variant the `bundled`
block of assets.json says it satisfies, every published variant of a
`required` asset must be carried by one of these files, and no file here may
be unaccounted for. That is the check that matters at install time. The device
enforces the digest itself, on a phone, and a file replaced here without its
catalogue row moving would otherwise reach a person as an artifact refused for
a digest with no explanation attached.

The four `bytes_` lines at the end of this file sum to 5757430, which is the
figure decision 0202 gives for what bundling adds to the package.

What is not here
----------------

python-toolkit, the fifth catalogued asset, is `on-demand` and has publication
`unpublished` on every variant with no path. There was nothing to fetch and
nothing was fetched. It stays a catalogue row with no bundled file, which is
the state the seeder must tolerate: `required` is what gets bundled, and an
`on-demand` artifact with no origin is simply never installed.

The non-Android variants — macos-arm64, macos-x64, windows-x64, windows-arm64
— are `unpublished` for every asset. TaffyGo is an Android product; those rows
describe a shape the catalogue can express and this product does not build.
