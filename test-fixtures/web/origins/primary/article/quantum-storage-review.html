How desk lighting affects focus

By Priya Raman · Published 2026-02-11 · Lumen Review (fixture publication)

Three months of desk measurements in a fixture office suggest that sustained reading comfort depends more on glare control than on raw brightness. This article is deterministic sample content; nothing here describes a real product or a real study.

What was measured

Each lamp was placed 40 cm from the page. Illuminance was read at the page centre, and a fixed reading passage was timed.

Conclusion

Diffuse light at roughly 600 lux produced the shortest reading time in this fixture. The sample is small and the passage was identical each time, so the result is illustrative rather than conclusive.
