National Records Office

The entrance to a multi-page errand. The document a task is asked for is three pages away and is reachable only by following a link from here.

The National Records Office keeps the civic register. Every service below is identified by a Civic Records Number: a twelve-digit number in the fictional Civic Records scheme, written as three groups of four.

Services

  • Request a records statement Needs your Civic Records Number, the characters in a picture, and the one-time code sent to your registered phone.
  • Request a records statement (checked by our verification partner) The same request, with the picture check replaced by a partner widget served from another site.
  • Update the address on your record An ordinary form with no identity check. Present so a task has to choose between entrances rather than take the only one.

Before you start

Every page of this errand is a deterministic fixture. The Civic Records scheme, the office and the statement are invented for the corpus, and no request made here is recorded anywhere.
