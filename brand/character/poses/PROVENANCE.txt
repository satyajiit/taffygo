Taffy character poses derived from brand/character/taffy-character-master.webp.

The generator overlayed a Grok wordmark in the bottom-right of every
JPEG. That mark is forbidden in product art. Each file here was rewritten
by covering the mark region with neighbouring background pixels so no
generator word, icon, or watermark remains.

These PNGs are exploration frames (often opaque / checkerboard), not
shipping Android UI. The app no longer packages a welcome cutout.

Do not ship a Grok watermark. If a later render carries one, strip it
before it touches src/main/res.
