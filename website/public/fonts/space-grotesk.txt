The two copies are deliberately different files, not a duplication to be
resolved: the Android UI needs the full variable TTF that Android's font
resources load, and the site needs a latin-subset woff2 small enough to be
worth self-hosting. Neither build can consume the other's format. What they
must share is the licence, and both carry it.

Self-hosting rather than linking a font CDN is a privacy choice: a webfont
request to a third party would tell that third party who visited the page, and
website/README.md records the rule.
