# See the Android app

Explore the bottom toolbar, separate task tabs, and light and dark themes in real app captures.

## The bottom row

Back, forward, Ask Taffy and your tabs sit in one row at the bottom of every page. Ask Taffy opens as a panel over the page you're on.

![The Ask Taffy panel with a request to find a Kindle Paperwhite's price on croma.com. Above the box Taffy says it may open up to 8 sites, that private details go only to the site, and that page text goes directly to your provider.](https://taffygo.com/screens/05-asks-before-acting-360.webp)

## Taffy's tabs stay out of yours

Tabs Taffy opens for an errand are grouped on their own in the tab switcher, and each one has an amber edge. You can turn your open tabs into a workspace from the same screen.

![The tab switcher: four of your tabs, then Taffy's tabs collapsed into their own group, with a note that an amber edge marks a tab Taffy opened for a task.](https://taffygo.com/screens/10-tabs-360.webp)

## Light or dark

TaffyGo follows your phone's light or dark setting. To choose one for TaffyGo alone, open Settings, then Appearance.

![The start page in the dark theme, with a request typed into the box and the Downloads, Workspaces, Tabs and Settings buttons along the bottom.](https://taffygo.com/screens/11-start-page-dark-360.webp)
