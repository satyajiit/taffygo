# Open-source licences

TaffyGo's own source is published under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. It is built on Chromium and other open-source software, each under its own licence.

## TaffyGo

The source is at [github.com/satyajiit/taffygo](https://github.com/satyajiit/taffygo) . [LICENSE](https://github.com/satyajiit/taffygo/blob/main/LICENSE) holds the licence text and [NOTICE](https://github.com/satyajiit/taffygo/blob/main/NOTICE) says what it covers. The name, the logo and the Taffy character are reserved, and [TRADEMARKS.md](https://github.com/satyajiit/taffygo/blob/main/TRADEMARKS.md) lists those files.

## The browser's notices

The notices for Chromium and everything else packaged in the app travel inside the app. In TaffyGo, open Settings, then About and help, then Licences. The list is generated from the build itself, so it names exactly what that copy of the app contains.
