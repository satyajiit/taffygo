# Hand Taffy an errand

An errand is a job that spans a few web pages: compare two listings, pull the times out of a recipe, find a price. This is one errand, captured on a phone from the request to the result.

![Taffy, the browser’s assistant](https://taffygo.com/cutouts/taffy.webp)

## One errand, three screens

- ### Say what you want Type the request in Ask Taffy and attach the pages Taffy may read. The panel says that those pages' titles and text go directly to your AI provider, and that no other pages are opened. ![The Ask Taffy panel with one recipe page attached and a question typed. A note says the page's title and text go directly to your AI provider and no other pages are opened.](https://taffygo.com/screens/x-ask-about-this-page-360.webp)

- ### Watch it work Taffy opens the pages in its own tabs and writes down what it is doing in plain words. Take over, Pause and Stop stay at the bottom the whole time. ![An errand running: Taffy compares a Kindle Paperwhite's price on flipkart.com and amazon.in. The screen shows the two pages, what Taffy is doing, and Take over, Pause and Stop.](https://taffygo.com/screens/04-errand-running-360.webp)

- ### Read the result The answer comes first, then what each page said, with its source. When a page left something out, the result is marked Partly done. Save it as a workspace, or put it away. ![The result of comparing a Kindle Paperwhite on flipkart.com and amazon.in: the lowest listed offer, ₹16,999 at Flipkart, then each listing's details with its source, marked Partly done.](https://taffygo.com/screens/06-results-360.webp)
