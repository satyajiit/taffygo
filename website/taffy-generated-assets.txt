TaffyGo website images
======================

Recorded: 2026-09-27
Owner: Matterward Labs Private Limited

Every image this website serves that is not a logo file from brand/ or the
official Google Play badge is listed here: the real phone screens, the
paintings of Taffy and the link-preview card. Each entry names the file, the
file it was made from, its pixel size, its byte count and its sha256.
tests/assets.test.ts holds every file under public/screens/ and public/art/,
and the card, to this record, so a file that changes without a new entry
fails the website lane.

website/scripts/build_media.py makes all of them from two directories kept
outside the repository, and can make them again:

  python3 scripts/build_media.py --screens <capture dir> --art <art dir>

Every painting shows Taffy, so each path below belongs in the reserved-media
table of TRADEMARKS.md beside the character master.

Phone screens
-------------

Method: captures of TaffyGo on an Android 16 phone (1268x2756 px, 520 dpi),
taken with tools/screens/capture on 2026-09-26, app version 152.0.7977.42.
Three were taken again on 2026-09-27 from a build of the same version: the
start page (01, 11), whose suggestion rows now carry their own labels, and
Backup (09), which no longer offers a restore. The status bar (156 px) and the navigation bar (52 px) are cut off, so each
screen starts and ends at the app's own edges. The pages shown are public:
allrecipes.com, amazon.in, flipkart.com and example.com. Every capture was
checked for personal data before use; none shows an account, email address,
phone number, saved password, browsing history, location or document.

Two captures are not the screen's resting state. The start page (01, 11)
has a request typed into its box, because the idle start page lists the
phone's most-visited sites. Nothing else was staged.

Derivation: the lossless cropped PNG, resized with Lanczos to 360 and 720 px
wide, saved as WebP at quality 82, method 6, with no metadata.

Paintings
---------

Method: painted in gouache style by an image-generation model, given the
approved character master (above) and one of the app's own task-scene
paintings as references. Each painting was chosen from three or four
candidates after checking Taffy against the master (face, hair streak, ears,
outfit and wings), five fingers on every visible hand, and no lettering or
marks anywhere. No painted pixel carries text. The prompts, every candidate
and the reasons for each choice are kept with the source files.

Derivation: the reviewed sRGB PNG, resized with Lanczos to two widths, saved
as WebP at quality 82, method 6, with no metadata.

website-hero: a person at a desk reads their phone while Taffy stands beside
their arm, ready. section-asks-first: Taffy holds up a blank card to a person
and waits for their answer. section-on-your-phone: a hand holds a phone whose
screen is a small room, where Taffy puts keepsakes on a shelf.

Link-preview card
-----------------

Method: a painting made the same way as those above (Taffy sits on the edge
of a desk beside a phone in a wooden stand), with the committed lockup
brand/png/taffygo-lockup-color-on-light.png trimmed to its opaque edges,
scaled and composited as pixels. No generated logo layer is involved: the
lockup is the committed file. Saved as a progressive JPEG at quality 86 with
an sRGB profile, and served byte for byte under a name carrying its hash.
